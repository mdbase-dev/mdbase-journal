# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

People capturing a note on a blank page. Every visit starts a page; text remains
ordinary Markdown in the selected collection. Local drafts preserve writing
independently of connectivity.

## Brand

Quiet, legible, restrained. Keep the existing Journal writing surface and mdbase
family identity; account controls support writing rather than competing with it.

## Anti-references

No dashboard, note list or decorative authentication card system. No internal
identifiers or protocol terminology in ordinary sign-in messages.

## Principles

- Preserve text and Undo independently of sign-in state.
- Keep pending entries pinned to their original destination across switches.
- Use the shared SDK lifecycle, not application-owned auth or polling engines.
- Native asset intake is not proof of native collection readiness or sync.

## Accessibility

Preserve semantic buttons/dialogs, keyboard shortcuts, legible light/dark themes,
visible focus and reduced motion. Render account metadata as text, never HTML.
